Our story

We cook from the heart

◆

Chleb i Wino is a place where simple ingredients meet real care in the kitchen. No pretence, no shortcuts — just good food in a beautiful room.

The place

The cellars of Villa „Prus”

The restaurant occupies the cellars of the historic Villa „Prus”, in the heart of the spa town of Solec-Zdrój. Exposed brick, white linen, candles and warm light make a room that suits a couple at dinner, a family over Sunday lunch and a larger party celebrating together.

In summer we also welcome guests in the garden — overlooking the spa park, with a calm that is hard to find elsewhere.

The entrance can be easy to miss from the street, as it leads down into the cellars of the building. If you are looking for us for the first time, head for Villa „Prus” on ulica 1 Maja.

The kitchen

Everything begins with the ingredient

Our fish does not arrive from a wholesaler. Zander, catfish and carp come from our own Wójcza Fish Farm — a freshness our guests can taste on the plate. Vegetables come from our own land, cheese from Strzałków, vinegar from Sandomierz apples, and beer from the small breweries of Ponidzie.

The menu changes with the seasons. We are not afraid to put tradition and modern cooking side by side — carp finds its way into dumplings, kimchi sits beside zander, and apple pie returns to the table deconstructed.

We are glad to cook for guests with particular dietary needs — just let us know when you book.
