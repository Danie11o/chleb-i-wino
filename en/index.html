A few words about us

Cooking from the heart, in the cellars of a historic villa

Chleb i Wino — „Bread and Wine” — is an intimate restaurant tucked away in the cellars of Villa „Prus” in the spa town of Solec-Zdrój. Brick vaults, white linen and candlelight make a room you want to linger in, long after the plates are cleared.

Our cooking is built on what is local and in season. The fish comes from our own Wójcza Fish Farm — zander, catfish and carp reach the plate fresh, never frozen. Vegetables come from our own farm, the cheese from Strzałków, and the beer from small breweries around Ponidzie.

We bake sourdough bread on the premises every morning. It has become our signature — and you can take a loaf home with you.
