Good to know

Frequently asked questions

◆

Do I need to book a table?

In high season and at weekends it is strongly advised — we are a small restaurant. Reservations are taken by phone on +48 41 357 68 57.

Do you have vegetarian dishes?

Yes. The menu includes the Vege dish, a cheese selection from Strzałków, cream of baby spinach and pappardelle with ricotta. Please mention any other dietary needs when you book and we will do our best to accommodate them.

Can I buy your bread to take away?

Of course. A whole 50 cm loaf costs 26 zł, half a loaf 17 zł. It is worth calling ahead to check availability.

Do you host private celebrations?

Yes — christenings, birthdays and company gatherings. The menu is arranged individually. Give us a call and we will put a proposal together.

Is there parking?

Yes — there is a car park directly next to the restaurant. No need to look for a space in the centre.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, pets are welcome. Do bring your four-legged companion — and if you would like to be sure of a suitable table, give us a call before you come.

Do you deliver?

We do not offer delivery. You are welcome to dine in or to collect a takeaway order.

Do you take card payments?

Yes. Prices are quoted in Polish złoty (zł / PLN).

* All prices shown on this website are indicative and subject to change — the menu card available in the restaurant is the binding one.
